MY PROJECT

CampusShelf

Making college library discovery easier for students.


CampusShelf is a website concept designed to help students discover books available in their college library without having to search through complicated catalogues or ask others for help.

The Problem

Students may not know which books are available, which subjects they cover, or where they are located inside the library.


My Proposed Solution

Easy Search

Search for books by title, subject or author.

Course-Based Browsing

Explore books according to course and semester.

Book Locations

Help students find the shelf where a book is located.

Current Status

I'm developing the concept and improving the website as part of my learning journey. The goal is to make library discovery simpler and more convenient.


Technologies explored: HTML, CSS, React and Express
